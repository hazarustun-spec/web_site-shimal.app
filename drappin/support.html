Drappin Support

Snap or upload an outfit photo — Drappin finds where to buy it.

Frequently asked

  • How many free scans do I get? 3 successful scans. Photos with no clothing don't count.
  • Why are some results blurred? Free accounts see the first 3 matches per scan. Drappin Plus unlocks every match, including the cheapest one we found.
  • How do I cancel Drappin Plus? iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Drappin. Refunds are handled by Apple.
  • How do I delete my account? Profile → Delete account. Everything is removed, including your Sign in with Apple link.
  • Pinterest doesn't connect. Only public boards are supported at the moment. Try disconnecting and connecting again.
