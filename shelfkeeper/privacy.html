Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Shelfkeeper ("we", "our", or "the app") keeps a record of the physical books in your home. This policy explains what data we collect, why, who we share it with, and how long we keep it.

1. Data We Collect

  • Nothing, until you sign in. Shelfkeeper works fully signed out. Used that way, your library never leaves your device and we receive no data about you at all.
  • Account information: If you sign in, your name and email address from Sign in with Apple (Apple may provide a private relay address instead of your real email), and Apple's stable user identifier for your account.
  • Your library: The books you add and what you record about them — title, author, publisher, year, ISBN, which room and shelf a book sits on, reading status, your notes, and the loans you record (the borrower's name as you typed it, the date lent and the date due).

We do not use advertising identifiers, we run no analytics, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

2. Photographs Stay on Your Device

Shelfkeeper can photograph a book's cover to identify it, and the inscription page inside a book to keep with the record. These photographs are never uploaded. They are stored on the device that took them and are not copied to our servers or to anybody else's.

When you photograph a cover, the text on it is read on your device. Only the words that were read — a title and an author — are sent to the book catalogues below. The photograph itself is not.

3. How Long We Keep Data

Your library is kept for as long as your account exists. Delete the account and it goes: the app has a Delete account button in the Profile screen that permanently removes your account, every book and loan recorded against it, and the photographs held on that device. This cannot be undone.

You can also write to us and we will do it for you.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Apple — Sign in with Apple provides your identifier and, if you allow it, your name and email.
  • Google Firebase (Firestore and Authentication) — stores your library and your sign-in session so the same shelf appears on your iPhone and your Mac. Used only when you are signed in.
  • Open Library and Google Books — looked up to identify a book. They receive an ISBN, or the words read off a cover. They receive nothing about you: not your name, not your account, not your other books.

5. Your Rights

You can see everything we hold about you inside the app — it is your library. You can correct any of it by editing the record, and delete all of it with the Delete account button or by writing to us. If you are in the EU or UK, you also have the right to ask for a copy of your data and to object to how it is processed; write to the address below.

6. Security

Data in transit is encrypted with TLS. Your library is stored under your own account identifier, and Firestore's rules refuse any request from another account to read or change it.

7. Children

Shelfkeeper is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

8. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes with it and the new version is published here.
