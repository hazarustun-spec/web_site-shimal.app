Shelfkeeper keeps a record of the physical books you own: what they are, where they sit, what you have read, and what you have lent to whom. Book details are looked up in public catalogues (Open Library and Google Books). Those catalogues are written by other people and are sometimes wrong, incomplete, or missing a book entirely — you can always correct a record or enter a book by hand.
