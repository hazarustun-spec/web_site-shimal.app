Your home library,
shelved.

A record of the books you actually own. What is on which shelf, what you have read, and who still has the one you lent last spring.

What it does

Scan the barcode

Point the camera at the back of a book. The title, author, publisher and year arrive on their own.

Or scan the cover

Older books have no barcode, and plenty of Turkish printings carry one that will not read. Photograph the cover instead: the type is read on your device and the book is found by what it says.

Know where it sits

Put every book in a room and on a shelf. When you say "the top shelf in the living room", the app knows what you mean.

Remember what you lent

Who took it, when, and when it is due back. The gap it left stays drawn on the shelf, so you can see the book is out of the house.

The story of the book

Where you got it, who gave it to you, what is written inside. Photograph the inscription and keep the note — that is what remains years later.

iPhone and Mac

Sign in with Apple and the same shelf appears on both. Signed out it works just as well; the library simply stays on the device.

Privacy, briefly

The photographs you take never leave your device. Book lookups send an ISBN or the words read off a cover — never your name, your account or your other books. No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no tracking. You can delete your account, and everything in it, from inside the app.
