A little more
about the game.

Bump Roll is a one-finger 3D puzzle that feels great to touch. To send a ball up, bring the bump underneath it; to send it right, come from the left. Every ball goes to the socket of its own colour, a wrong socket ends the level, and walls stop the bump too — so you have to think about the route, not just the push.

Free to play with occasional ads between levels. Rewarded ads are always optional, and a single purchase removes the ads between levels for good.

01

One finger, no tilting

Touch beside a ball and a bump rises under your finger. Push it where you want it. That's the whole game.

02

A new twist every few levels

Gates, dark holes, sliding hatches, portals, key balls, numbered sockets, painters, turnstiles, heavy and light balls, tethered pairs, merging balls.

03

Short and satisfying

60 hand-made levels across five candy-coloured worlds, each timed for three stars. Pool-table knocks, crisp haptics, a glossy toy look.
