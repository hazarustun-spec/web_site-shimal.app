BUMP ROLL

Accessibility

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Bump Roll is played with one finger and needs no reading or fast reactions to understand. Here is what the game supports today, and what it does not yet.

Supported

  • Colour is never the only cue. Every ball colour has its own shape (ring, triangle, square, star, plus, keyhole), printed on the ball, inside its socket and in the progress pips at the top. You can match balls to sockets by shape alone.
  • Sound and haptics are optional. Sound, music and vibration each have their own switch in Settings; nothing in the game depends on hearing a sound.
  • One-finger control. Touch and hold anywhere on the board; there are no gestures, swipes or multi-touch.
  • Large touch targets. Buttons respond to an area of at least 44 × 44 points (version 1.1 and later).
  • Eight languages in the game, chosen automatically from your device language or in Settings (version 1.1 and later).

Not supported yet

  • VoiceOver and Voice Control: the board is a physics scene and is not described to screen readers.
  • Larger Text and Dark Mode settings: the game uses its own fixed text size and colour themes.
  • Reduce Motion: some celebration effects (confetti, bouncing sockets) still play.

Each level has a clock, but running out only means a retry, and a rewarded "+15 s" or a level skip is always available.

Feedback

If something makes the game hard to play for you, please tell us at hello@shimal.app. Accessibility fixes go first in our updates.
