Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

In short: Classic editing happens on your device. When you choose AI enhancement, a downscaled copy of your photo is processed to generate a lighting guide and is not stored on our servers. No ads, no tracking, no third-party analytics, and we never sell your data.

1. Who we are

Bring Light ("the app") is an iOS photo-editing application operated by Shimal (shimal.app) ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we process, why, and what choices you have. A Turkish version is available at gizlilik.html.

Questions? Contact us at hello@shimal.app.

2. Information we collect

Sign in with Apple

Sign in with Apple is required to use AI enhancement. When you sign in, we receive a stable Apple user identifier and — only if you choose to share it — your email address, which may be an Apple private relay address. Authentication and account records are stored with Supabase in the EU (Frankfurt region).

Data stored per account

  • Your Apple user identifier and, if shared, your email address.
  • A daily AI usage counter (to apply free-plan limits).
  • Subscription status: product, original transaction ID, and expiry date.
  • An Apple refresh token, kept only so we can revoke it when you delete your account.
  • Request log metadata: timestamp, AI model name, latency, and whether the automatic quality check passed or failed.

We do not store your photos in your account or anywhere on our servers.

3. How your photos are processed

  • You select photos using the iOS system photo picker. The app only receives the photos you pick.
  • Classic editing happens entirely on your device.
  • When you tap AI enhancement, a downscaled copy of the photo (maximum about 1024 px, with metadata and GPS location stripped) is sent over an encrypted TLS connection to our server, hosted on Vercel (Frankfurt region), and forwarded to Google's Gemini API to generate a lighting guide.
  • We do not store the photo on our servers. It is kept in memory only for the duration of the request.
  • Google processes the image under the Gemini API paid-service terms, which do not use paid API content to train Google's models.
  • The final result is computed on your device from your original photo.
  • When you save, the edited image is added to your Photos library as a new copy; your original is left unchanged.

4. How we use information

  • To authenticate you and provide AI enhancement.
  • To enforce free-plan limits and unlock Premium features based on your subscription status.
  • To monitor reliability and quality of the AI feature using request log metadata.
  • To revoke your Apple sign-in token when you delete your account.

5. What we don't do

  • No advertising.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No third-party analytics.
  • No sale or sharing of your data for marketing.

6. Service providers

  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, and subscriptions via In-App Purchase (StoreKit). Payments are handled by Apple; we never receive your payment details.
  • Supabase — authentication and account records (EU, Frankfurt region).
  • Vercel — hosting of our server that relays AI requests (Frankfurt region).
  • Google (Gemini API) — generates the lighting guide from the downscaled photo copy.

7. Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple. To determine your subscription status we store the product, original transaction ID, and expiry date. Manage or cancel your subscription in iOS Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions.

8. Account deletion and data retention

You can delete your account at any time inside the app from the Settings screen › Delete account. Deletion is immediate: your account records, usage counter, subscription status, and request logs are deleted, and your Apple token is revoked. Photos are never retained on our servers, so there is nothing further to delete.

Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription — please cancel it separately with Apple in iOS Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions.

9. Your rights (GDPR / KVKK)

Depending on where you live, including under the EU GDPR and Türkiye's KVKK (Law No. 6698), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete your data (also available instantly in the app).

To exercise these rights, email hello@shimal.app. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

10. Security

Data in transit is protected with TLS. Access to account records is limited to the services that need it to operate the app.

11. Children

Bring Light is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest version. Material changes will be communicated in the app or on this page.
