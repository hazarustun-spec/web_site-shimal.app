How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings › [your name] Apple ID › Subscriptions, select Bring Light, and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid renewal. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a subscription.

How do I restore my purchases?

Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe, then open the paywall in Bring Light and tap Restore Purchases. Your Premium access will be reactivated.

How do I delete my account?

In Bring Light, open the Settings screen and tap Delete account. Deletion is immediate: your account records are removed and your Sign in with Apple token is revoked. Active subscriptions must be cancelled separately in iOS Settings.

Why does AI enhancement need an internet connection?

Classic editing works fully offline on your device. For AI enhancement, a downscaled copy of your photo (location and metadata removed) is sent securely to our server and to Google's Gemini API to generate a lighting guide. The photo is not stored, and the final result is computed on your device from your original. See the Privacy Policy for details.

What's included in the free plan?

Unlimited classic editing and 3 AI enhancements per day, with a watermark on AI results. Premium removes the daily limit and the watermark.
