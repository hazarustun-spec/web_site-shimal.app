IN-APP EVENT / ZOO MERGE

Halloween in the Zoo

For one week a year, the zoo dresses up.

From 24 October to 1 November the game puts on its costume by itself — no update to download, nothing to turn on. Open it and it is already there.

Open Zoo Merge ↗Free to play · Optional in-app purchases

What changes
while it lasts.

The three skies behind the pen turn to lantern-lit dusk, moonlight and a purple night. Three of the ten animals come dressed as the season — a pumpkin, a bat and a ghost — each keeping the colour of the tier it stands in, because colour is how you tell them apart at a glance. The music changes with them, and a few bats drift across the menu while you decide whether to play one more.

01

Pumpkin skies

Three painted backdrops that take over the pen for the week.

02

Three animals in costume

A pumpkin, a bat and a ghost stand in for three of the chain — same colours, same merges.

03

Merges that throw sparks

Every third shard of a merge comes out pumpkin or purple, and the big ones send two bats off into the dark.
